About

I'm Aviad Shahar Tendler — an e-commerce brand strategist who believes that the best brand positions aren't invented, they're discovered. My work sits at the intersection of market data, consumer insight, and strategic thinking. I help founders and brand leaders stop guessing and start building on a foundation of evidence.

Most brand consultants start with aesthetics. I start with data.
